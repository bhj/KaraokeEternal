---
title: Why isn't pitch shifting available?
weight: 8
---

Pitch shifting runs in the player, and browsers require a secure (HTTPS) connection to enable it. To serve Karaoke Eternal over HTTPS, run Karaoke Eternal Server behind a reverse proxy — see <a href='{{< ref "docs/karaoke-eternal-server#reverse-proxy-https" >}}'>Reverse Proxy (HTTPS)</a> to get started, including example configurations (Caddy, NGINX, Traefik, Cloudflare Tunnel and Tailscale).
